The Phase System

Colour that follows the body — every phase gets its own gentle identity.

Menstrual

DAY 1 – 5

The body lets go. Energy is lowest — rest, warmth and gentle movement take the lead. Logging flow and cramps here is quick and quiet.

#D4899A · #FAE0E4

Follicular

DAY 6 – 13

Oestrogen rises. Energy climbs, mood lifts, focus sharpens — the phase for new projects, creative work and strength training.

#A8C5A0 · #E4F5EE

Ovulation

DAY 14 – 16

Peak energy and confidence. Communication flows easily, stamina is highest — the golden window for goals, social energy and high-intensity workouts.

#F0C87A · #FEF8E8

Luteal

DAY 17 – 28

Progesterone rises. The body prepares and slows — cravings, sleepiness and sensitivity are expected. Supportive, low-pressure content steps in.

Voice & Tone

Concise, warm, action-oriented. Never clinical, never alarming.
Calm Supportive Empowering Warm Honest Breathing Personal Gentle
Do
  • Mirror the user's words back ("You logged cramps")
  • Frame predictions as gentle estimates, never facts
  • Use phase-appropriate, actionable wellness tips
  • Give one clear action per screen
Don't
  • Never use alarming or medical-red messaging
  • No jargon — "follicular phase" explained in plain words
  • No guilt, shame or judgment around missed logs
  • No invented stats without a source

Accessibility

Inclusive by default — calm, legible and never excluding.
Contrast that stays calm
All body and label text meets WCAG 2.1 AA contrast on the warm neutrals. Phase accents are used as fills and indicators — never as foreground text on white, where lighter tones like ovulation gold would fall below ratio.
#D4899A
#A8C5A0
— fills only, not text
Safe reds, never alarming
Danger (#C0392B) is reserved for destructive actions — it is never used to flag normal cycle data or variations. No flashing, no strobing, no disorienting motion.
Focus that's visible
Every interactive element shows a clear 2px focus ring in rose on a light halo. Keyboard navigation flows left-to-right through Home, Cycle, Wellness, Log, Profile.
Reduced motion respected
The interface honours prefers-reduced-motion — phase transitions become instant cross-fades, and all animation is disabled for vestibular comfort.

When the cycle phase changes, the interface colour shifts gradually — rose to green to gold to lavender — as a gentle, continuous gradient rather than a snap. This keeps the "the app changes with your body" idea feeling organic, not jarring.

Design Case Study

From research to hi-fi prototype — the thinking behind Aureah.
Most cycle tracking apps feel clinical, alarming, and generic. They show dates and predictions — but never explain what those dates mean for how you feel, how you should eat, or how you should move. Aureah was designed to fix that. It's a calm, supportive companion that adapts its content, language, and colour to the user's current cycle phase — delivering the right guidance at the right moment, in the right tone.
The Problem
Existing apps treat cycles as data — not lived experience. Users feel overwhelmed, not supported.
The Vision
A phase-aware wellness companion. Calm over clinical. Empowering over alarming. Personal over generic.
The Outcome
33+ hi-fi screens, a full design system with phase accent tokens, and an interactive Figma prototype.

Research — Key Insights
Key Insight 01
"I never know what phase I'm in"
Users open their current app and see a date. Not a phase name, not an explanation, not a feeling. They feel disconnected from their own body data.
Key Insight 02
"The advice is always the same"
Generic wellness content doesn't account for cycle phase. A HIIT recommendation during menstrual phase is not just unhelpful — it's actively discouraging.
Key Insight 03
"Logging feels like a chore"
Multi-step logging flows with clinical language create friction. Users stop logging after 3–5 days. The log needs to feel like a journal, not a form.
Key Insight 04
"I feel judged when I miss a day"
Streak-based guilt ("You haven't logged in 3 days") actively harms engagement. Aureah uses encouragement language — "Welcome back, log when you're ready."

Design Principles

Four rules that guide every screen, every word, every colour.
01

Calm over clinical

Warm tones, never alarming reds. The interface feels like a supportive friend, not a doctor's office.

02

Breathing whitespace

Generous padding, never crowded. Every element has room to breathe — clarity through space.

03

Phase-aware colour

Accent colours map to the menstrual cycle phase. The interface changes with the user's body.

04

Serif + sans pairing

Playfair Display brings editorial warmth and trust. Open Sans keeps the UI clean and legible.
